# Table-level personal-record exclusions

- `Beteiligung von Mitgliedern an Geschäften des Grossen Rates` (groups g0117–g0119): excluded the entire table. Each row links an identified parliamentary member UID to an individual member's participation role and a record date.
- `Vorstösserinnen und Vorstösser im Grossen Rat des Kantons Thurgau` (groups g0154–g0159): excluded the entire table. Rows represent individual parliamentary initiatives/proposals with signatory status and identifiable proposal titles.
- `Anfragestatistik des Data Shop im Statistischen Amt des Kantons Zürich ab 2015` (groups g0276–g0282): excluded the entire table. The examples show individual dated enquiries, including private-person request type, channel, effort, and staff code.
- No other adopted table was judged to be a table of individual personal records. The image catalogue includes a photographer field, but its rows describe archival image objects rather than people; that field was independently labeled `person`.
